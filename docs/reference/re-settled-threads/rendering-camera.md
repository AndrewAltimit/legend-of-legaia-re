# Settled threads: Rendering / camera

One area of the [settled reverse-engineering threads](../re-settled-threads.md) register.
The evidence grades (`disassembly` / `capture` / `decompiled-C` / `inference`) are defined on
[the index page](../re-settled-threads.md#the-evidence-column).

This area covers how a frame gets on screen: the per-prim mesh dispatcher and its handler tables, the
view-matrix build and camera framing, the GTE light path, fades, fog, drop shadows and the
screen-effect layer, plus the battle and minigame shots that script their own camera. Each row pins
one rendering question a port or a retail-frame comparison depends on; the working detail lives on
[`renderer.md`](../../subsystems/renderer.md) and the subsystem pages each row links.

### Does retail stack coincident curved shells?

*Status:* resolved (no) - Evidence: `capture`.

Every surface in the live ordering table is submitted exactly once, so retail has no stack of
coincident shells to order.

- **Measurement:** field-run display-list reads in `jouind` (1263 packets walked) and `jouine` (1016)
  report zero screen-coincident surface groups above a 16 px^2 area floor, with no undecoded polygon
  link in either state.
- **Decoder requirement:** `POLY_FT3` carries seven payload words. The two chains hold 46 and 45
  textured flat triangles - the strand meshes `jouine` is built from - and a walk that skips them is
  blind to exactly those.
- **The one coincidence:** a single mesh drawn three times in one texture family
  (`clut=0x7F86 tpage=0x001F`). That is the multi-pass semi-transparency idiom, not a mesh stack: its
  members share a material, and two different env TMDs would not.
- **Consequence:** the coplanar kernel's same-position curved-shell residual is a property of how the
  port assembles a scene's env TMDs, not something retail resolves by ordering.
- **False positive 1:** retail double-buffers, so ordering tables come in pairs holding frame N and
  frame N-1 with near-identical packet counts; merging a pair makes every surface look stacked with
  itself.
- **False positive 2:** distant geometry projects to 1-3 pixel slivers that coincide constantly
  without saying anything about meshes, which is what the area floor is for.
- **Coverage limit:** each read is one frame and one camera. The library's other `jouine` / `jouind`
  entries are byte-identical backups of these two (a library filename is the sha256 of its
  contents), and `chitei2` is unmeasured and inherits the conclusion. The negative rests on scale: a
  stacked shell would double many adjacent surfaces at once, and in cave interiors the walls are the
  dominant on-screen geometry.

### Does any retail shot author a non-zero camera roll?

*Status:* resolved (yes) - Evidence: `capture` for the census (a disc-derived executing oracle),
`disassembly` for the factor.

Slot `2` of the op-`0x45` CONFIGURE mask is the roll angle `_DAT_8007B794`, the third factor of the
camera build `FUN_80026988`'s `Rx * Ry * Rz`, and eight scenes stage a reachable, executing non-zero
value.

- **Factor:** `0x8001CFD0..0x8001CFE8` loads `_DAT_8007B794` (`lui a0,0x8008; lh a0,-0x486c(a0)`)
  and calls `0x8004638C` unless the render node's `+0x52` bit `0x200` is set.
- **Writers:** none of `FUN_801DAB90`, `FUN_801DB8EC` or `FUN_801DBE9C` writes `_DAT_8007B794`; the
  only zeroing is the scene-entry reset `FUN_80025C24`.

  trio, focus X and Z, `H`, and no focus Y (the `opdeene` reading on
