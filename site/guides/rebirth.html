Legend of Legaia: Rebirth

The flagship expansion pack for the port. Rebirth gives levelling to 99 a purpose: Genesis Tower, an endgame climb of new fights and superbosses tuned for a late-game party, a quest chain that threads them through the world you know, custom maps built from the disc's own scenery, trading cards won in battle and traded or sold, and modes the original never had. It is also the worked example of the format - nothing in the engine knows the pack by name, so anything Rebirth does, another pack can do.

The story

On the cliffs past Rim Elm stands Genesis Tower, a sea tower older than the Mist. The islanders say its foundation stone was cut from a root of the first Genesis Tree. Its bell once called the fishing boats home through the fog, until the Mist's first breath came in off the sea and the last Warden, Ysolde, sealed it under that stone and bound herself there to hold it. The bell has been silent ever since, and Rim Elm half forgot the tower.

Now the Genesis Trees are pushing the Mist back, and what is left of it sinks toward the tower, carrying what it swallowed. Each floor has a keeper that will not let go. The party climbs to make the Mist release the tower floor by floor, crosses to the Lantern Isles behind its sea gate, and at the top meets the Hollow Sovereign. The last step is Ysolde's own: once someone stands ready to meet the First Breath, she can let go of her seal.

The climb

The quest chain admits each area at a party level, and every keeper's parting line names the level the next one asks for. The fights are tuned for those levels, and a test (crates/engine-expansion/tests/rebirth_balance.rs) checks it on the battle kernels at Normal, Hard and Nightmare: each keeper falls in under a dozen rounds, nothing one-shots a full-HP member, and each floor's keeper outweighs the last.

How to play it

  1. Get the packIt comes with the game: the release archive carries it as packs/rebirth.lxpack, and the play page has it built in. From a checkout, bundle it yourself (asset expansion bundle packs/rebirth -o rebirth.lxpack), or open the workbench, load the Rebirth example and Download .lxpack - or press Play in browser and skip the file entirely.
  2. Load itDesktop: the launcher's Expansion pack... button. Browser: the play page's Expansion pack card, once your disc is loaded.
  3. Play to the towerContinue a late save, or start a new game and play to it. The way in is an entrance in Rim Elm that opens for a party of level 30 or more. From that level the Journal's Travel page (Select on the pause menu) lists the tower too, so you can go straight there from wherever the save stands; once you have been on a map, Travel takes you back to it from anywhere. To look around sooner: --map genesis_1f on the desktop, the pack's section of the play page's Scenes list, or the Unlock all Travel maps cheat.
  4. Follow the JournalSelect on the pause menu: the quest chain, the challenges with their best scores, your cards.
# Native, straight onto the tower's first floor
legaia-engine play-window --disc "Legend of Legaia (USA).bin" \
    --expansion packs/rebirth --map genesis_1f

How it is built

Rebirth is written with exactly the files and tools any modder uses: plain TOML, checked by the same validator, editable on the same workbench. Opening it on the workbench is the quickest way to see how each piece is made.

PieceHow it is writtenReference
Endgame monstersRetail monsters with scaled stats and new names; the engine reads their meshes and moves off the disc.monsters
Boss AIEach boss's own script: phases at HP thresholds, telegraphed ultimates, guards summoned into empty seats, an enrage timer, a last stand - every move the base monster's own swing or a disc spell.boss scripts
A gauntlet fightA formation whose later monsters arrive in waves as seats free up.waves
Levels 60 to 99A banded XP curve over retail's steps, multiplied EXP and gold, pack enemies that scale with the party.progression
The quest chainQuests gated on party level and on each other, with dialogue lines and a once-only superboss.quests
The towerA map painted on the workbench's tile painter over a retail scene's scenery, with props, zones and an entrance in a retail town.maps
The card setCards whose portraits are retail monsters, boosters, a dealer and drop rules on retail and pack fights.cards
ModesA poker table, a duel, a golf course, a race track and a prop-hunt arena, each a few lines of numbers and layout.modes
