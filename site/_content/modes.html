Game modes

The port has game modes the 1998 game never had: Texas Hold'em, the Grid Duel card game, mini-golf, checkpoint racing and prop hunt. Pick one below and play it right here. No disc needed: the rules are the port's own, and they run as the same WebAssembly the play page runs.

Here the games are drawn flat. To play them in 3D, at a table in the tower with the cards, chips and players in the world, or on a course laid out on the map, choose Rebirth as the rules and press Play in 3D on a mode. That opens the play page with your disc, which draws the world. On the play page itself, the same list is under Game modes in 3D in the scene picker once Rebirth is loaded.

What these are

Every mode is a rules engine of the port's own, written so that two machines fed the same button presses stay in step: no floating point in the game state, one seeded random generator per session, and a state digest the netplay layer compares. An expansion pack picks the numbers and layouts - the poker table's blinds, the golf holes, the race track, the cards in the duel deck - in its modes.toml and cards.toml. Practice set is the one instance of each mode the engine carries for a game with no pack; Rebirth is the flagship pack's own set. The engine docs are Expansion game modes.

The screens here are the ones the game draws, panel for panel. Only the lettering differs: the in-game font is read from your disc, so this page sets the labels in a web font instead. A few Rebirth modes are laid out on a pack map - the golf links, the circuit and the two tables in the tower. In the game you walk that map while you play; here the same session draws as its top-down screen. To play them on their map, open the play page with your disc, choose Rebirth in the expansion controls and pick the mode under Game mode.

Nothing you win here is kept: a page session has no save file. In the game, poker winnings go to your gold or casino coins and card rewards to your collection.

Playing online

Online rooms run inside the full game. Golf, racing and prop hunt play in lockstep (every machine runs the session from the same presses). Poker and Grid Duel run on the host, who sends each player only what their seat may see. A browser joins a room from the play page's Online panel; the native play window (--host) or a legaia-relay server hosts it. See Playing online for the steps.
