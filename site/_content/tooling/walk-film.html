The field walk film: one walk, both sides, pass by pass

The retail comparison corpus scores one frame per state, so it cannot see how a walk unfolds: how fast the follow camera closes, which way the pad turns the player, what a script carrying the player does to the camera's ease. The walk film holds one pad schedule on one retail field state on both sides and compares them pass by pass. It reports and asserts nothing; its output reads retail RAM, so it stays under captures/.

At a glance

scripts/ci/walk-film.py kor_field_route_walk --pad 0-180:UP --frames 200 --run
scripts/ci/walk-film.py kor3_field_route_walk --pad 0-120:UP --no-retail   # engine side only

--run sets the Field Move option to Run. Read it off the retail side's step - a run step is 24 at frame step 2, a walk step 16 - since the seed has no host to mirror the option from.

Reading it

  • Pairing. A retail pass is a row where the player or the camera changed. The engine walks one vsync at a time and eases on the pass grid, so each retail pass pairs with the last engine row on the same spot, and the summary counts the camera fields that differ. A low pairing count means the two walked different paths: read that first.
  • The pose. Both sides write the player's clip, its cursor and its rate. Retail's cursor steps once a pass, the engine's once a vsync at half the step, so the two meet on every second row.
  • The first pass differs by construction. A state saved mid-walk has one more pass of the old pad queued.
  • Placements. The placement report keys on the flat MAN index. A wanderer's heading and seat are rand() history, so one that parts from retail after a few dozen vsyncs is not a finding by itself; a placement off retail from the first row is.

What it has settled

  • The follow camera's ease runs once a game pass, not once a vsync (encounter and camera); a player a script carries reads as still, and the camera focus trails a pass.
  • The d-pad remap reads the scene's authored octant, not the live camera angle.
  • kor3's placements hold retail's headings for a whole film, with no spin.
  • Placements that turn in place under an idle pad turn on both sides: rugi_field_card_boot's four and cave01_attached_light's five hold retail's heading on every vsync of a 300-vsync film, and vell_fog_field's one turns at retail's rate a vsync ahead, the seed's pass parity rather than a spin. A sweep of every field state finds no other placement turning in place.
  • A player a script carries up a stair glides onto each step as retail's does, -32, -8, 0 a pass, instead of landing on it at once, and the leg spends on the game-frame grid, so the follow focus trails it a whole pass; on tower_field_walk that brought the camera's pitch, eye height and focus onto retail's for nearly every pass.
  • A placement at geremi_field_door_hop that looked mis-faced is not: the scene's system script turns it every pass onto the bearing of a wanderer, so its heading is that wanderer's rand() history one pass late, on both sides.
  • An ambient script's turns run at retail's rate and differ only in phase: two placements repeat retail's ramps a fixed number of vsyncs early, the wait their scripts were inside at the capture.
  • kor_field_route_walk's displayed frame is its pass-two-back pose exactly, filmed over the passes before the save by autorun_walk_route.lua with LEGAIA_FILM_SCENE.
  • Town placements hold retail's headings and seats for a 300-vsync film in garmel, vozz and retona; town01_field_card_boot's seven that part are wanderers, each first off after 40 vsyncs or more.
  • A held walk's clip runs at retail's rate: kor_field_route_walk steps 16 a pass through the same ten frames on both sides.
  • kor3's stair warp lands the camera on the destination block. With the state's queued first pass replayed, the engine walks retail's path into the warp tile, lands a vsync apart, stands on retail's shot from the landing on, and turns the player by the octants the room selector moves, to retail's heading. One thing still parts: the slide along the wall starts a step early, since the engine resolves the direction every vsync and retail once a pass.
  • concnow_field_door_hop's camera word climbs on both sides: the door-hop script's ramp is 240 steps long, where the engine had posted its end at once. The two still end apart, because each reads its footing on a different phase of the floor bob.
  • Idle 300-vsync films of ten more fields (retock, taiku, nilboa, jou and others) read no placement off retail. Where placements did part with nothing pressed, each turned or stepped at retail’s rate one vsync early or late: the game frame's phase, which the film now seats (below). What still parts is dolk2's flat 70 and wanderers.
  • dolk2_market_noa's flat 70 is the state's history, not a spawn defect: the spawner seats it on its header's tile, which is the engine's seat, and the state's position is where a scene script's NPC run left it. A film seeded from the scene's spawn does not replay that beat.
  • A beat's last turn holds when the engine plays the beat: in son the film from the mid-beat checkpoint ends on retail's heading and seat for Luctes.

The game frame's phase

Retail's game pass runs once every frame step of vsyncs, and a save state is taken on one vsync of that frame - not necessarily the first. The frame pacer (FUN_80016B6C) waits each frame out with libetc's VSync(step), which stores the vblank count when it returns. The state's live vblank counter less that store is therefore how far into its frame the state stands, and the next pass opens step - phase vsyncs into the film.

Seating the phase is a change to the comparison harness, not to the game code. It is what moved the placements that had turned a vsync early or late onto retail's vsync: conc_field_card_boot, conc2_field_route_walk and town01_field_card_boot now read no placement off retail, and dolk2_market_noa is down to its flat 70.
