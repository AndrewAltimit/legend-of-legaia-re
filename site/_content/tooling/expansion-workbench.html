Expansion workbench

Build an expansion pack for the port in the browser: new monsters and bosses, a level curve, quests, dialogue, trading cards, game modes and custom maps, checked against your own disc as you edit and exported as one shareable .lxpack. Everything runs in this tab as WebAssembly - the disc never leaves your machine and nothing is uploaded. New to packs? Read what an expansion pack is and the step-by-step guide.

A pack carries no game data. It names the disc's monsters, scenes, meshes and music by number, and every player supplies their own disc. ?

The names in the pickers (monsters, items, scenes) are read from the disc you load here and shown to you only. A picker writes the coordinate - { kind = "monster", id = 109 } - never the name; a pack holds a name only where you type one.

Every export runs the validator's no-disc-bytes rule. Reference: docs/formats/expansion-pack.md.

Find ?

Every place in the pack a value sits. An id (a monster, a line, a quest, a map, a pack flag) lists where it is defined, set, read and referenced; monster:109, item:5, scene:town01 or bgm:12 list a retail coordinate's users; anything else is a text search over keys and values.

Empty, it lists the pack's flags and counters with how often each is set and read: a flag read and never set is a gate that never opens. Click a place to go to the field. Ctrl+K or / focuses the box.

Checks ?

The pack validator, re-run a moment after every edit against the pack exactly as it would be exported, with your disc resolving every coordinate. An error stops the engine loading the pack and stops the download; a warning does not.
