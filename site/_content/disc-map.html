What the boxes are

The outer square is the whole Mode 2/2352 image, one unit of area per 2048-byte sector. Its children are the ISO9660 directory extents and files in disc order: the boot configuration, the SCUS_942.54 executable, CDNAME.TXT, the PROT.DAT asset archive, the CD-XA audio streams, the STR movies and the DMY.DAT fixture archive, plus the system area ahead of the volume descriptors and any sectors no directory record claims. See disc geometry.

Inside PROT.DAT, the first boxes are the header and TOC and the two raw TOC rows below extraction entry 0; the rest are the 1233 TOC entries, each sized by the sector gap to the next entry (PROT.DAT TOC), grouped by the CDNAME block they belong to. An entry's index is its extraction index - the NNNN in extracted/PROT/NNNN_*.BIN - and its block is resolved in the raw-TOC frame, two rows ahead.

An entry's colour is the format family of the class the workspace categoriser assigns its bytes (legaia_disc_audit::categorize). That says what an entry is, not how much of it a parser consumes - byte accounting answers that. Grey boxes are padding, filler slots and entries no named detector claims yet.

The map is drawn from disc-map.json, layout metadata only - names, LBAs, sector counts and class names, no disc bytes - written by asset disc-map <disc.bin> --out site/disc-map.json.
