Scene names

What is balden? Every scene on the disc is addressed by a short developer label from CDNAME.TXT - town01, balden, vozz - that says little about where you are. This page is the one table that names each of them: the name every page on this site shows, the banner the game itself draws when you walk in, and the in-game reading the table was cross-checked against.

At a glance

What it is
One row per CDNAME.TXT block, in disc order, from data/gamedata/scenes.toml.
Ground truth
The scene's own banner - the name the game draws on entering it and writes into a save slot (place names, site 3). A disc-gated test holds every banner in the table to the disc byte for byte.
Where a banner is shared
Thirteen Sol scenes all say "Sol Tower"; the table adds a qualifier - Sol Tower (stairs A) - so no two playable scenes share a name.
Who reads it
The play page's scene picker, the asset viewer's scene filter, the world and NPC pages, and gamedata-tool list scenes.
Contributor
Scene identifications by Stann0x; his reading of each scene is kept in its own column.

The slots that crash when loaded

Six blocks cannot be entered as scenes, for two different reasons:

  • opkorout, opmap01 - every entry of both blocks is a single 2048-byte pochi placeholder sector. There is no map, no asset bundle and no scene script, so a loader pointed at them decompresses filler.
  • other4, other5, other6 - minigame data (the slot machine, Baka Fighter, the Muscle Dome's ringside stills) that the minigame overlays load by path. They carry no walkability map, so they are not scenes at all.
  • other7 - scene-shaped, but a developer leftover: an earlier revision of koin3's scene script that nothing in the retail game loads.
