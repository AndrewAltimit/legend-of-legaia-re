Battle action helpers

The battle action state machine owns the order of a turn and almost none of its arithmetic. Range tests, damage rolls, the escape roll, AI picks, effect and summon spawns, camera framings and voice cues all live in helper routines it calls, or that run beside it each frame. This page is the map of those helpers: what each reads and writes, and the constants it carries.

The helper families

Range metric

FUN_8004E2F0 answers “is the attacker close enough” for the attack chain and for the animation tick’s root-motion gate. It returns a 16-bit distance, and zero means in range.

  • The position read is asymmetric: the attacker’s live pair +0x34 / +0x38 against the target’s seat pair +0x3C / +0x40.
  • A monster’s size class is its record byte +0x1F. Small classes compare against the thresholds {256, 384, 1024} at DAT_80078870; a larger class compares against size << 4, unsigned.
  • Each party member carries a reach offset at DAT_80078878: {+43, 0, -53, -100} for roster ids 1 to 4. It is added to the distance, so a positive value is a shorter reach.
  • A target code the law reads as out of range is how the flee staging walks the party away: the walk’s root motion never finds its target.

The two damage-roll wrappers

Every capture-class hit goes through one of two sibling wrappers. Each draws an attacker roll and a defender roll, applies the element-affinity scale FUN_801DD864, then the closed-form finisher FUN_801DDB30.

WrapperStat mixed into both rollsFifth finisher argumentParty resist ladder
FUN_801DD4B0INT-working +0x1680runs: jewels, elemental guards, All Guard
FUN_801DD6B4ATK-working +0x1581skipped

The fifth argument is the resist bypass: the finisher tests it once (bne a3,zero at 0x801DDBD4) and jumps past the whole party-defender block. That is the mechanism behind the boss casts that ignore elemental guards. The affinity scale still reads the caster’s element either way. Formulas: battle formulas.

The status word

One actor halfword, +0x16E, carries every condition. Its bits are pinned by the HUD icon selector’s priority ladder and by the appliers that set them.

BitsCondition
0x0001 / 0x0002Venom / Toxic
0x0004Stone
0x0008 / 0x0010 / 0x0020Rot, one rolled limb
0x0380AI delegation, always set as a group
0x0400 / 0x0800Numb / Sleep
0x1000Curse
0x2000 and upunused: no applier in the dumped corpus

Three masks are read as units: 0xF84 gates a slot out of the selectable scans, 0x0F80 is what taking damage clears, and 0x0404 is the whole-actor inert test. There is no KO bit: a dead actor is one whose HP halfword is zero, which is why every consumer pairs the two.

The escape roll

FUN_801E791C decides a flee. Both sides score from speed plus how hurt they are, and each rolls rand() % score:

party_score = sum over party   (SPD * 3) >> 1 + (maxHP - curHP) >> 4
enemy_score = sum over enemies  SPD           + (maxHP - curHP) >> 5
Escape Boost:  roll_p += roll_p >> 1
Great Escape, a pre-emptive strike, or the forced-flee flag:  roll_p = roll_e
FAIL iff  not forced  and  (roll_p < roll_e  or  ctx[+0x287] != 0)
  • “Assured” describes the compare, not the outcome. Great Escape forces a tie so the compare cannot fail, but the scripted-fight flag ctx[+0x287] is tested afterwards and still blocks the flee. That is why the Chicken King is assured escape from non-boss fights.
  • ctx[+0x287] is not a counter-attack byte. Battle setup derives it as (DAT_8007BD60 >> 5) & 4, so it reads 0 or 4.
  • The pre-emptive latch ctx[+0x291] is re-copied from a cell the first round clears, so a pre-emptive strike’s unfailable compare lasts one round.
  • On success the routine also stages the scene: the party turns its back, takes an out-of-range target and walks off; downed members are floored at 1 HP in their records.

AI picks and the turn budget

  • The companion watches the wrong actor by design. FUN_801EED1C’s character-id-4 arm reads seat 0’s HP and status and writes seat 1’s queue. Its physical leg redraws rand() % monster_count + 3 until the seat is alive.
  • Auto and Rage members get their queue from the auto arts-combo assembler FUN_801F0450, which fills the same byte string a player would enter.
  • Monsters queue several actions out of an agility budget (FUN_801E9FD4); a delegated or confused monster re-rolls its target through FUN_801E7320 at the turn seed.

The queue itself is on the action queue page.

Camera and pose

FUN_801D5854 is the pose driver: one presentation program per pose id 0..9, called with the acting slot. Three of its arms read the per-action style byte ctx[+0xD] through the same compare ladder: values 1 and 3 add half a turn of yaw, 2 and 3 tilt the pitch by 0x80 and store TR.y = 0x400. FUN_801D829C arms the eased tween every framing change goes through. The framings themselves are on battle stage and camera.

Keeping the party apart

Nothing walks a combatant home after an action. Three helpers, plus the round start’s recentre, are what keep a formation in frame and its members out of each other’s way.

  • The seed recentre (FUN_801EFE44) runs for every category but Run. It squashes a span over 0x800, then boxes only an Attack’s target (or its whole side for the group codes) and subtracts the box’s centre from every included actor, so a melee opens with its target on the stage origin. The round start’s FUN_801DB318 does the same over the whole formation.
  • Empty party seats count. Retail keeps a struct behind every pool slot, so in a solo fight the two empty party seats move with the formation and weigh in every later box. The port keeps their ground positions for exactly that reason.
  • The make-way step: state 0x14 gives each bystander a heading a quarter turn off the attack line, on whichever side it already stands, and seeds a countdown from the melee monster’s size class. The action SM’s shared tail then steps those actors each pass until the countdown drains. Without it, an approach runs straight through a partner standing in the lane.
  • The separation pass (FUN_80050BB8, driven by FUN_80051078 after the action SM) reads each pair’s radii - the seat stager writes 0x280 on a party seat, 0x400 on Terra’s, a monster’s size class << 5 - and nudges both live positions apart when their body pairs sit closer than a sixth of the summed radii. Neither routine reads the frame step, so the port runs it once a battle frame on either cadence.

Ports: battle_action::seed_formation_recentre, arm_make_way and make_way_step in engine-battle-vm, and battle_separation::push_apart driven by World::tick_battle_separation.

Audio and voice

  • _DAT_8007B910 is the live audio level, not screen brightness. The summon and capture arms ramp it down and back; two of those duck arms sit behind the scripted-fight flag and never run in an ordinary encounter.
  • Battle voices are CD-XA clips played through FUN_8003D53C: a grunt per normal move, a shout per Tactical Art chosen by FUN_8004C140, and a fanfare per Hyper Art.
  • FUN_801F3990 fires the item and Spirit voice cue from the wait state 0x3D.

The overlay also carries its own small PRNG (FUN_801D0290). It is a shape hash for the lightning ribbon emitter and feeds no gameplay roll - those draw the BIOS rand.
