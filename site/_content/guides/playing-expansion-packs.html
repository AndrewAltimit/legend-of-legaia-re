Playing an expansion pack

Load a pack, start a game, and the pack's content sits inside the world you know: entrances in retail towns lead to its maps, its quests show up in a Journal on the pause menu, its fights and cards turn up in battle. You need your own Legend of Legaia (NA) disc and the pack; the pack carries no game data of its own.

On the desktop

  1. Pick the packThe launcher's Expansion pack... button takes an .lxpack file, or the pack.toml inside a pack folder. Dropping either on the window works too.
  2. It is checkedThe launcher runs the same validator the game does. A pack with errors is refused and the help box lists the first few; a good one shows on the button by name, with its version and authors in the help box.
  3. PlayNew Game and Continue now play with the pack. The choice is remembered (expansion = "..." in launcher.toml); Delete or a right-click on the button goes back to no pack.

The launcher hands the pack to the game as --expansion, which works by hand too:

# New Game and Continue play with the pack
legaia-engine play-window --disc "Legend of Legaia (USA).bin" --boot-ui \
    --expansion packs/rebirth
# Straight onto one of its maps (a folder or an .lxpack)
legaia-engine play-window --disc "Legend of Legaia (USA).bin" \
    --expansion rebirth.lxpack --map rebirth_tower_1f [--spawn dais]

With --resume-save, a save made on one of the pack's maps lands back on that map instead of --map.

In the browser

On the play page, load your disc first: a pack activates against it. The Expansion pack card then takes an .lxpack; its map list and Enter map walk you straight onto one of the pack's maps, and Unload goes back to the game as it shipped. Installed packs stay in the page's pack library, each with its cover, version and authors; a pack that needs a pack you have not installed, or conflicts with one you have, says so under its row (one pack plays at a time). The expansion workbench's Play in browser button installs the pack it is editing in that library and opens the play page on it; End on the play page reloads the library's copy in place.

The browser takes the bundled .lxpack form only. A pack kept as a folder becomes one with asset expansion bundle, or on the workbench (load the folder, Download .lxpack).

The Journal

  • Quests lists the started quests, active first, with each objective's progress and the reward.
  • Challenges lists the challenges with their best scores; Cross arms one for the next battle.
  • Cards shows the trading-card collection and opens the card menu (binder, boosters, selling, the duel deck).
  • Travel enters any of the pack's maps directly - the way in for testing a map whose entrance is out of reach.

The pack's name and version head the screen, with the count of new cards.

Saves

A save made with a pack carries that pack's progress - quests, pack flags, challenge records, beaten bosses, the map you stood on, your cards - in a block of its own, and reloads it. A save made without a pack has no such block and is the same bytes it always was.

  • A save naming a pack that is not loaded still loads, says so ("Saved with expansion 'rebirth' - not loaded"), and keeps the pack's progress to write back on the next save, so playing a while without the pack loses nothing.
  • A save made on a pack map returns to the map with the player where they stood; without the pack it lands in the map's base scene.

The block's layout is on quests and saves.
